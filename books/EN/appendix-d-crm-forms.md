# Appendix D — CRM Forms

Fill-in forms that mirror what a CRM records. Use them on paper or copy them into
your CRM as fields. The point is the same: capture the fact now, while it is
fresh, so nothing is lost to memory. Leave the cells blank and fill each one as
you go.
