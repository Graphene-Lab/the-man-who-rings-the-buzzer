# Appendix E — Recommended Reading

A short, curated shelf. Every book here is real and well known. Read one at a
time; a book you finish beats a list you admire. Grouped by what it teaches.

## Selling: classics and craft

- **How I Raised Myself from Failure to Success in Selling** — Frank Bettger
  (1950). A life story that proves selling is a learnable skill, not a gift.
- **How to Win Friends and Influence People** — Dale Carnegie (1936). The
  original on warmth, listening and likability — the base of all rapport.
- **The Psychology of Selling** — Brian Tracy (1988). Practical mindset and
  habit-building for the working salesperson.
- **Secrets of Closing the Sale** — Zig Ziglar (1981). The classic on asking
  for the order with warmth and persistence.
- **SPIN Selling** — Neil Rackham (1988). The question model — Situation,
  Problem, Implication, Need-payoff — for big, considered sales.
- **The Little Red Book of Selling** — Jeffrey Gitomer (2004). Short, punchy
  reminders on attitude and daily discipline.
- **The Ultimate Sales Machine** — Chet Holmes (2007). Pig-headed discipline
  and the dream-100 target list — close cousin to the buzzer method.
- **Fanatical Prospecting** — Jeb Blount (2015). Why filling the pipeline is
  job one, and how to do it without fear.
- **The Challenger Sale** — Matthew Dixon & Brent Adamson (2011). Teaching and
  reframing beats pleasing; a strong counterweight to pure relationship selling.
- **To Sell Is Human** — Daniel H. Pink (2012). A fresh look at selling as
  attunement, buoyancy and clarity for everyone, not just salespeople.
- **Predictable Revenue** — Aaron Ross with Jason Lemkin (2011). The
  cold-email and sales-division system behind modern outbound pipelines.

## Persuasion and psychology

- **Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion** — Robert B. Cialdini (1984).
  The foundational book on the core levers of why people say yes.
- **Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade** — Robert B.
  Cialdini (2016). What to do *before* the ask; adds unity as a key lever.
- **Yes!: 50 Scientifically Proven Ways to Be Persuasive** — Noah J. Goldstein,
  Steve J. Martin & Robert B. Cialdini (2008). Small, tested tactics in short
  bites.
- **Thinking, Fast and Slow** — Daniel Kahneman (2011). How the mind really
  decides; the science behind intuition and bias.
- **Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth, and Happiness** — Richard
  H. Thaler & Cass R. Sunstein (2008). Choice architecture — how framing
  steers decisions.
- **Made to Stick** — Chip Heath & Dan Heath (2007). Why some ideas survive and
  others die; useful for your message and your pitch.
- **Contagious: Why Things Catch On** — Jonah Berger (2013). The mechanics of
  word-of-mouth and social sharing.
- **Building a StoryBrand** — Donald Miller (2017). Put the customer at the
  centre of the story you tell.
- **Hooked: How to Build Habit-Forming Products** — Nir Eyal (2014). Why
  products keep users coming back; good for value and retention thinking.

## Negotiation

- **Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In** — Roger Fisher &
  William Ury (1981). Where BATNA comes from; principled negotiation.
- **You Can Negotiate Anything** — Herb Cohen (1980). A practical, streetwise
  guide to bargaining in any situation.
- **Bargaining for Advantage** — G. Richard Shell (2006). A structured,
  honest framework for preparing and negotiating your own style.
- **Start with No** — Jim Camp (2013). Why chasing "yes" weakens you, and how
  to negotiate from calm, not neediness.
- **Never Split the Difference** — Chris Voss with Tahl Raz (2016). Tactical
  empathy and mirroring from an FBI hostage negotiator.
- **Difficult Conversations** — Douglas Stone, Bruce Patton & Sheila Heen
  (1999). How to handle the talks you are afraid of.
- **Crucial Conversations** — Kerry Patterson, Joseph Grenny, Ron McMillan &
  Al Switzler (2002). Keeping dialogue safe when stakes and emotions are high.

## Marketing and growth

- **Marketing Management** — Philip Kotler & Kevin Lane Keller. The standard
  textbook; the full map of modern marketing.
- **Marketing 4.0: Moving from Traditional to Digital** — Philip Kotler,
  Hermawan Kartajaya & Iwan Setiawan (2017). The shift from traditional to
  digital, and the customer journey.
- **Marketing 5.0: Technology for Humanity** — Philip Kotler, Hermawan
  Kartajaya & Iwan Setiawan (2021). Using data and technology to personalise
  without losing the human touch.
- **Permission Marketing** — Seth Godin (1999). Earning attention instead of
  grabbing it; the opposite of spam.
- **This Is Marketing** — Seth Godin (2018). Marketing as service to a
  specific person with a specific need.
- **Traction: How Any Startup Can Achieve Explosive Customer Growth** — Gabriel
  Weinberg & Justin Mares (2015). The Bullseye framework for finding growth
  channels that work.
- **The Lean Startup** — Eric Ries (2011). Build-measure-learn; the discipline
  behind experimentation and A/B testing.

## Mindset and the long game

- **The Power of Habit** — Charles Duhigg (2012). How habits form and change —
  the engine behind daily sales discipline.
- **Shoe Dog** — Phil Knight (2016). The Nike founder's memoir; showing up,
  persisting, and loving the work — the buzzer spirit in book form.
