## What this book is about

"The Man Who Rings the Buzzer" is both a literal tool and a metaphor: the courage and
discipline of showing up in person, in a world that hides behind email. Across **44
chapters and 5 appendices**, it takes the reader on the full journey of the
buzzer-ringer — from mindset and preparation, through the visit and the pitch, to
follow-up, negotiation, measurement, the science of persuasion, and selling across
cultures.

### What you will learn

- **Foundations** — what cold B2B selling really is, the essential vocabulary, and the
  mindset to handle rejection.
- **Strategic preparation** — segmentation, the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), buyer
  personas, territory management, and prospect research.
- **The visit** — the art of the buzzer, the 30-second pitch, storytelling, discovery,
  presenting value, handling objections, and closing for the appointment.
- **After the buzzer** — follow-up and nurturing, negotiation (BATNA, ZOPA), closing,
  after-sales, and referrals.
- **Measurement** — KPIs, CRM, and data-driven pipeline analysis.
- **The science of persuasion** — Cialdini's 7 principles, body language,
  micro-expressions, eye contact, and the voice.
- **Schools of marketing** — relationship, experiential, inbound, account-based,
  growth hacking, Marketing 4.0/5.0, neuromarketing, and value-based selling.
- **Strategies around the world** — the East (guanxi, nemawashi), Europe, and the USA,
  with Hofstede's and Hall's cultural models.
- **System, ethics, and growth** — GDPR and privacy, building and leading a sales team,
  and stories from the field.

Every chapter ends with **exercises** and a **summary**, and includes a real,
reproducible **"AgentBridge in action"** example that shows how an AI agent helps a
seller win — from researching a prospect to producing a proposal, a report, or a
follow-up.
